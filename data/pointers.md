# DNS data

The DNS data are not redistributed here and are available from the original sources.

- **Square duct** (Re_b = 5000, 11386; aspect ratios 3, 5, 7): R. Vinuesa, P. Schlatter, H. M. Nagib, *Phys. Rev. Fluids* 3, 054606 (2018). <https://www.mech.kth.se/~rvinuesa/DuctData/index.html>
- **Square duct** (Re_b = 4410, 7000, 17800, 40000): S. Pirozzoli, D. Modesti, P. Orlandi, F. Grasso, *J. Fluid Mech.* 840, 631–655 (2018). <http://newton.dma.uniroma1.it/square_duct/>
- **Periodic hills** (alpha = 0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.25, 1.5): H. Xiao, J.-L. Wu, S. Laizet, L. Duan, *Comput. Fluids* 200, 104431 (2020). <https://github.com/xiaoh/para-database-for-PIML>. The alpha = 1.0 case is the configuration of M. Breuer, N. Peller, Ch. Rapp, M. Manhart, *Comput. Fluids* 38, 433–457 (2009).
